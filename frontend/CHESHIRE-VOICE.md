# Darker Light Astrology — Cheshire narrator

The chosen direction is one warm, sly, British fantasy narrator: a lovable smart-ass with a smile in his voice. Humor is affectionate. Tease cosmic bureaucracy and the absurdity of being human; follow each joke with a clear explanation or useful question. The rabbit is a quiet visual guide along the path, with no dialogue or separate performed personality. All spoken narration belongs to this one guide voice.

This is an original character performance with a Cheshire-like mood. The site currently still uses the device narrator. No ElevenLabs account, designed voice, generated recording, or paid service is connected yet.

## Set up the voice

1. Create your account at https://elevenlabs.io/sign-up and complete sign-in yourself.
2. Open **Voices → My Voices → Add a new voice → Voice Design**.
3. Paste the description below into the voice prompt, and the sample into **Text to preview**.
4. Listen to the previews. Save the one that feels right as **Darker Light — Cheshire Guide**.

The product keeps one narrator. Previewing alternatives here is a one-time production decision, not a voice menu for visitors. Generating previews uses ElevenLabs credits; the site has not purchased anything or started a subscription. These steps are based on the official guide: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-design.

## Voice description

Native English, British English. Male, middle-aged. Studio quality. Persona: charming, mischievous woodland guide. Emotion: amused, warm, quietly self-assured. A velvety mid-low voice with a smile behind the words, gently musical intonation, and unhurried conversational pacing. Dry humor lands through a knowing pause and a lightly lifted eyebrow in the delivery. He is a lovable smart-ass: playfully irreverent, perceptive, and kind underneath. Clear, natural pronunciation of astrology terms. Keep longer explanations relaxed and easy to follow. Teasing feels affectionate, never contemptuous. An original fantasy character with an intimate storytelling presence.

## Text to preview

Ah. There you are. A little red coat between the trees. Follow the rabbit when you’re ready. There is no prize for rushing. Astonishing, I know. Take a look at your chart. Those little symbols are planets; the numbered doors are houses. Every door opens onto a different part of life. Start with the Moon. It offers a way to reflect on what helps you feel settled, and what you reach for when life gets noisy. A useful thing to know before sending that extremely well-considered text at midnight. No judgment. Well, a little. Affectionately. Shall we?

## Once the voice is saved

Export a short MP3 or WAV sample for review before replacing the site's narrator. Static welcome clips can be shipped as audio assets; readings containing a visitor's calculated sign, house, and degrees need a secure narration service to keep that same voice throughout. Keep service credentials on a server, never in the frontend or in chat. Any future cloud narration should explain what reading text is sent and continue to exclude private journal notes.
