# Enchanted forest scenery

`enchanted-forest-rabbit-hole.png` was created with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-11, using `wonderland-rabbit-hole.png` as the edit target. It supplies the entrance, chart-map and general destination backgrounds, plus The Threshold Reading's door illustration. The original art remains in the project.

The zodiac, fireflies, moving trail and chart interaction remain independent code layers. Constellations painted in the tunnel are decorative, not calculated placements.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: wide 16:9 painted background for the existing Darker Light Astrology interactive Rabbit Hole website. Input image 1 is the existing environment to revise. Primary request: make this setting much more enchanted forest, retaining its lush classic animated-film / Wonderland storybook style and the magical astrology rabbit hole. Replace most floating cards, teacups and oversized clocks with a deep emerald and teal woodland: ancient curving trunks and moss-covered roots framing both sides, arching leafy canopy, luminous ferns, delicate wildflowers, a few glowing mushrooms, drifting warm golden fireflies and soft moonbeams through layered distant trees. Keep one subtle small hanging pocket watch and a tiny warm doorway among roots as whimsical accents. Integrate a luminous spiralling violet-teal rabbit hole into the forest floor in the lower center-right, around x62%, y70%, its rim made of curling roots, with faint star constellations in the depths. Painterly dimensional cel-animation finish, inviting and mischievous enchanted woodland, not horror. Deep forest greens and turquoise with violet accents and warm amber points. Preserve composition suitable for web overlays: upper middle and central region relatively calm and dark enough for cream copy and a centered birth-details form; rich detail around edges, visible trees and canopy even when center is covered. No characters or rabbits, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Our approved red-waistcoat rabbit, interactive zodiac symbols, interface and all calculations are separate layers and must not be painted into the image.

## Chase treatment

The current experience deliberately removes large front-facing rabbit portraits. A brief red-coat trace and golden glint move ahead and disappear into destinations. The original `rabbit-guide.png` remains saved, but is not rendered by the active journey. A requested rear-view raster variant was blocked by image generation and is not used.
