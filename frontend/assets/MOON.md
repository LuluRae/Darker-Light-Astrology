# Moonlit Conservatory

## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: wide background painting for an interactive astrology story room, no user-interface elements.
Primary request: a dreamlike moonlit conservatory deep down an Alice-in-Wonderland rabbit hole, a polished whimsical animated-film environment with soft rounded sculpted shapes and rich painterly detail.

Composition: landscape 16:9. Three clear story objects: a tall ornate oval SILVER MIRROR at the left quarter around mid-height, a tiny welcoming golden arched DOOR within a whimsical tree on the right quarter around mid-height, and an oversized delicate porcelain TEACUP on a low mossy pedestal in the lower middle-right foreground. Clear open stepping stones in the lower middle-left foreground for a separately composited rabbit guide. Deep layered perspective, curved shapes and gently impossible scale. Upper middle has open sky, objects visible and distinct with breathing space.
Lighting/mood: enchanted, tender, quietly curious, cinematic rim light, velvety midnight indigo and smoky lavender, watery teal, warm ivory and antique gold.
Constraints: environment only, no people or animals or faces, no text, no symbols or numbers, no watermark, no flat vector look, no interface panels. Rich cinematic illustration, not a photograph.
