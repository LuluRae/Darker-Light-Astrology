# Wonderland setting

`wonderland-rabbit-hole.png` was created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. It is used as scenery in the opening, vortex, and destination scenes. Motion, rabbit animation, interactions, text, and chart calculations are separate application layers.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full-bleed background art for an interactive Alice in Wonderland inspired astrology rabbit-hole experience. Create a richly illustrated classic 2D animated storybook environment, wide landscape 16:9. The viewer stands in a fantastical midnight garden at the edge of a huge glowing rabbit-hole vortex. Giant red spotted mushrooms and twisting teal foliage frame left and right; eccentric hanging gold pocket watches, tumbling playing cards and porcelain teacups; a small crooked ornate red door suspended in the far right middle distance, impossible curved checkerboard steps spiral down into a deep luminous purple and blue tunnel centered around x60%, y60%. Painterly cel animation, beautiful confident outlines, dimensional color shading, lavish illustrated detail, theatrical depth, whimsical sophisticated fairytale atmosphere, saturated emerald teal, violet and ruby red, warm amber glows and cream. Composition: central upper-left zone (roughly left 15-55%, top 10-35%) dark uncluttered blue-violet sky for webpage text overlay. Vortex rim lower middle for an animated rabbit sprite added by code. Detail concentrated around edges, tunnel visually compelling. NO characters or rabbits in the image (our animated white rabbit in a red waistcoat will be added separately), NO text, no logos, no UI, no photorealism, no flat geometric mockup. This should feel like an exquisite frame from a hand-painted animated Wonderland film.
